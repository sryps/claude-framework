## Report a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a security problem.

Report it privately:

- GitHub: use "Report a vulnerability" on the Security tab of this repository (private vulnerability reporting).
- Email: {{security@example.com}}. Encrypt with our key if you can: {{link to PGP key}}.

Include:

- A description of the problem and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- Affected versions, endpoints, or files.
- Your name or handle if you want credit.

## What happens next

- We confirm receipt within {{3}} business days.
- We give a first assessment within {{10}} business days.
- We fix critical issues as fast as we can and keep you informed.
- We publish an advisory after a fix is available, and credit you if you agree.

## Safe harbor

We do not take legal action against good-faith research that:

- avoids privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption,
- uses only accounts you own or have permission to test,
- gives us reasonable time to fix the problem before you disclose it.

## Out of scope

- Denial of service and volume testing.
- Social engineering and physical attacks.
- Findings from automated scanners with no proof of impact.
- {{Third-party services we do not control.}}
